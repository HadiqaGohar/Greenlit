### Scene 2: Script Input (30 seconds)
*Paste sample script*

**Sample Script Text**:
```
EXT. TITANIC DECK - NIGHT, APRIL 14, 1912

JACK stands at the bow, looking out over the Atlantic. 
The ship's captain, EDWARD SMITH, approaches wearing his 
regulation White Star Line uniform.

JACK
Captain, I heard we're making record time to New York.

CAPTAIN SMITH  
Indeed. At this speed, we should arrive Tuesday morning, 
April 16th. The wireless operator just received a message 
about ice warnings from the SS Californian.

Jack checks his iPhone for the time: 11:40 PM.
```

**Narration**:
> "I'm pasting a scene from a historical drama. Notice the mix of accurate historical details and obvious anachronisms - perfect for testing our fact-checking."

### Scene 3: AI Processing (45 seconds)
*Show loading animation and explain the backend*

**Narration**:
> "Behind the scenes, Gemini Enterprise Agent extracts factual claims - historical dates, character details, technical specifications. Each claim is then researched in real-time using Parallel API for live, accurate data."

**Show**:
- Film reel loading animation
- Processing steps explanation
- Technical architecture overlay

### Scene 4: Results Analysis (90 seconds)
*Walk through the production notes*

**Expected Claims**:
1. ✅ **Verified**: "Titanic sank April 14, 1912"
2. ✅ **Verified**: "Captain Edward Smith was the captain"  
3. ❓ **Uncertain**: "SS Californian sent ice warnings"
4. ❌ **Flagged**: "iPhone in 1912" (Anachronism)
5. ✅ **Verified**: "White Star Line uniform details"

**Narration**:
> "The AI identified 5 factual claims. Three are verified with sources, one needs manual review, and one is flagged as anachronistic - iPhone didn't exist in 1912! Each claim shows confidence scores and research sources."

**Actions**:
- Click through each claim
- Show inline script highlighting  
- Demonstrate claim filtering
- Highlight sources and confidence scores

### Scene 5: Production Impact (30 seconds)
*Explain real-world benefits*

**Narration**:
